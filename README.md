# Project Introduction

It is intended for AI enthusiasts who hold academic ambitions—planning to pursue a master's degree at a "Project 985" university in China, and subsequently a doctoral degree at a top-tier artificial intelligence institution in Europe, with the ultimate career goal of becoming a professorial senior engineer.
